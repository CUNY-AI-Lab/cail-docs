Course Access

Enter your course workspace through the Lab and keep its maps separate from your other projects.

Workspaces

Systems Mapping provides a general workspace for signed-in Lab members and a course workspace for each approved course. Each course keeps its own maps, links, and prompts. An item saved in one workspace remains there when you open another.

Students choose a course under Your courses on the Projects page or follow an instructor's ?course= link. The server checks the Lab's enrollment records on every request, so a withdrawn student loses access even if they retain the link. A course owner can use instructor controls for that course. A general Lab administrator receives no course-owner override, and ownership of one course grants no access to another.

What an Instructor Sees

ItemVisible to the course instructor?
Roster and first/latest visitYes, from admission records and the course workspace.
Saved progress (level and stage per map)Yes. The instructor sees the revision, save time, and stage.
Map content, memo, evidence, Field NotesThrough a submitted revision or an eligible project link shared with them.
Unsubmitted maps without a shared linkNo.
General-workspace mapsNo.

The course roster shows progress metadata so instructors can see who has started and which level they have reached. Students choose when to submit a revision for review. An instructor who receives an eligible project link can also view that linked map under the same sharing rules as other course members.

Guest Instructors

A course can grant a guest instructor specific permissions to open the instructor panel, read the roster, or read and edit prompts. The grant does not require course ownership, student membership, or administrator access. The app checks the relevant permission on every request.

Demo Course

The CAIL Teaching Tools Demo course supports workshops and try-outs. The Lab issues revocable invitations tied to verified CUNY identities. Demo maps follow the same privacy rules as maps in other courses.
