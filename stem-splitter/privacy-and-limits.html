Privacy & Limits

Personal work stays private to your account. Course work gives the instructor access after you acknowledge the course policy, and shared folders give classmates the access you select.

Original uploadOwner onlyOwner onlyOwner only

Students must check the acknowledgment on the Splitter tab before the app assigns new work to a course. Earlier work stays personal. Instructors can read course conversations without generating an answer, editing the transcript, or forwarding it.

Retention

The app deletes uploaded audio and stems 90 days after the split. Public listening links expire at the same time. A shared folder keeps an expired split as a placeholder so the surrounding work retains its context; the folder does not extend the recording's lifetime.

Delete removes a split's stems, notes, and conversation immediately. Download any stems you need before you delete the split or its retention period ends.

Daily Allowances

Settings shows your remaining split runs and Listening Guy turns, together with the reset time. Both reset at midnight UTC, which falls at 8:00 PM Eastern during daylight time and 7:00 PM during standard time. Successful splits count toward the allowance. A failed or cancelled split releases its place.

Generated guides and existing conversations cost no turn to read. Settings also estimates the separate Lab model allowance that covers use across CUNY AI Lab tools.

Sources and Licensing

The app accepts MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, OGG, and AIFF uploads up to 100 MB. Use music you are entitled to use for class. Internet Archive search returns public, downloadable MP3s with music metadata and a credited source. The app applies a duration limit that gives verified students and instructors more time than visitors. Metadata alone does not establish permission to reuse a recording.

For a YouTube link, the app looks up the video's title and suggests an Archive search. That path does not download the YouTube audio.

Guest Access

When the Lab enables guest access, visitors can make a small number of splits each day within one browser session. The guest's work stays private to that browser and follows the same retention rules. After signing in, the visitor can claim the work into an account. Course features require a signed-in member.

Reporting a Problem

Report a problem in the footer opens a form for your description. The report includes your verified account name, the page, and the app release. It excludes audio, notes, and conversations. For access questions, email ailab@gc.cuny.edu.
